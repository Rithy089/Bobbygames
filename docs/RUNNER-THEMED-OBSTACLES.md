# Angkor Runner setting obstacles

The five fictional running settings share jump, slide, and dodge rules. Each new setting starts with one center-lane obstacle and an action arrow before returning to two blocked lanes with an open coin path. The riverside favors woven market baskets (jump) and carts (dodge); the fictional courtyard uses a planted sandstone trough (dodge) alongside slide gates; the green trail uses tied bamboo (jump). The Angkor vista keeps the natural trail props, with more slide gates and carts. None of the obstacles represents a real temple, sacred object, national flag, or historic reconstruction.

## Original artwork

These sprites were generated specifically for BobbyGames with the built-in image generation tool, trimmed, resized to 420 pixels wide, and encoded as transparent WebP. No third-party assets or license obligations apply.

| Asset | Role | Source prompt summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/sprites/runner-baskets-v1.webp` | Riverside jump | Two handwoven palm-leaf market baskets with tropical produce and checked cloth; single isolated obstacle, painterly Cambodian-inspired arcade art, transparent background. |
| `public/sprites/runner-planter-v1.webp` | Courtyard dodge | Fictional freestanding sandstone garden planter with tropical greenery; no sacred architecture or temple carving, transparent background. |
| `public/sprites/runner-bamboo-v1.webp` | Green-trail jump | Tied bundle of freshly cut green bamboo poles with small leaves; matched to the basket sprite's lighting and transparent alpha. |

Prompts requested original, single, front three-quarter view props with crisp outlines and no scenery, text, logos, people, flag, or real monument. The bamboo's successful generation used the basket output as a style and alpha reference. Only these final transparent assets are used; earlier draft sheets with opaque backdrops were rejected. Game sprites remain lazy-loaded with the runner route.
